# 01 Exchange

> 01 Exchange (01.xyz) was a full-suite decentralized derivatives exchange on Solana offering cross-margined, cross-collateralized Perpetual Futures and Power Perpetuals (SQUOL), built on a fully on-chain Serum limit orderbook. The people and product behind 01 are now part of N1 (app.n1.xyz); trading, points, and rewards have migrated there. This profile captures 01 Exchange's historical developer surface (SDKs and program interface published by the Zero One Global Foundation).
